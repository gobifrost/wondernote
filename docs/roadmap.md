# WonderNote roadmap

The agent-only foundation is intentionally small and domain-neutral. Future work
should arise from observed collaboration needs without adding a fixed CRM,
project-management, or journaling taxonomy.

Potential follow-ups:

- Concept merge and alias-management tools for genuinely duplicated entities.
- Richer relationships between records without adding new record types.
- Optional notifications for sharing-access changes.
- Space rename, archive, and explicit ownership transfer when collaboration
  needs them.
- Additional import adapters kept outside the agent's permanent tool surface.
